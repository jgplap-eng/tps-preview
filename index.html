Group Reformer levels

Which reformer class is right for you?

Our group classes are split into three levels so first-timers learn properly and experienced clients keep progressing.

Foundation

Start with the essentials

Reformer setup and safety, breathing and alignment, core engagement. No experience required.

All Levels

Build confidence and progress

Continuous flow, more movement combinations and deeper cueing. Recommended after two Foundation classes.

Intermediate

Challenge your control

Faster sequences and complex combinations for clients confident on the reformer. Recommended after ten All Levels classes.

Compare the three levels

Before your first class

Questions, answered

Where is Tessavi Pilates Studio?

On the second floor of WOW World Complex inside the Subic Bay Freeport Zone, Zambales. It is a short drive from the Olongapo City gates. Directions and map.

Is this the only Reformer Pilates studio in Subic?

Tessavi was the first Reformer Pilates studio to open in Subic Bay and is the only studio in Subic offering STOTT PILATES®. Group classes are capped at four people, each on their own reformer.

I have never done Pilates. Where do I start?

Book a Foundation Group Reformer class or a private session. Foundation covers reformer setup, breathing, alignment and core engagement. No experience is needed.

How much is a Pilates class in Subic Bay?

A single group reformer class is ₱1,100; a 6-session group package is ₱5,700. Private sessions start at ₱2,250 and duo sessions at ₱1,600 per person. See all rates and packages.

How do I book?

Pick a slot on our live timetable, or message us on Facebook Messenger and we will book it for you. You can also call or text 0961 692 3951.
