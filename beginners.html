Learn the reformer

Setup and safety, breathing and alignment, core engagement, then the first footwork and bridging series.

What the hour feels like

Slower than you expect, harder than it looks

Foundation classes move deliberately. You will spend the first minutes learning how the carriage, springs and footbar work and how to get on and off safely. Then comes breathing and finding neutral spine, the position everything else is built on.

The exercises themselves look simple: footwork, bridging, arm work with the straps, a short abdominal series. Done with control, they are surprisingly demanding. Expect to feel your deep core switch on, sometimes for the first time.

Because there are never more than four people in the class, your instructor can adjust springs, give hands-on corrections and slow things down whenever you need it.

After class one

Your next three months

Two Foundation classes are usually enough to move to All Levels. From there, two or three sessions a week is the sweet spot; a 12-session package over two months fits that pace and includes a complimentary basic facial at Tessavi Clinic. After roughly ten All Levels classes, your instructor will invite you to Intermediate.

Prefer to learn one-on-one first? Many beginners book one or two private sessions and then join the group.

FAQ

Beginner questions

Do I need to be flexible or fit to start?

No. The reformer meets you where you are: springs can assist a movement as easily as they can load it. Foundation classes are built for people who have never done Pilates.

What do I wear?

Fitted leggings or shorts and a top that will not ride up when you are upside down. Grip socks are required on the reformer and are available at the studio.

Should I eat before class?

A light snack one to two hours before is ideal. Avoid a heavy meal right before; a lot of the work is on your back and stomach.

Can I come if I have back pain or an injury?

Often yes, and many clients come for exactly that reason, but tell us first. We will recommend a private session so the instructor can modify everything safely. Please follow your doctor's advice.

Is Pilates suitable during pregnancy?

With medical clearance and a private session, usually yes. Message us so we can schedule you with the right instructor and adapt the programme.

How quickly will I notice results?

Most clients feel taller and more connected to their core within three or four sessions. Visible strength and posture changes typically follow eight to twelve consistent sessions.
