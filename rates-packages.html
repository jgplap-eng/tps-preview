FAQ

Pricing questions

How much is Pilates in Subic Bay?

At Tessavi a single Group Reformer class is ₱1,100, a private session ₱2,250 and a duo session ₱1,600 per person. Six-session packages bring the group rate to ₱950 per class.

How long are packages valid?

Single sessions and 6-session packages: one month. 12 sessions: two months. 24 sessions: three months.

Can I share a package with a friend?

Group and private packages are personal. For two people training together, the Duo package is priced per person and you book the same slot.

What is the free facial?

Selected 12-session Group and Duo packages include one complimentary basic facial at Tessavi Clinic, our sister skin clinic in Subic.

How do I pay?

Packages and single classes are purchased through the online timetable at the time of booking. Ask us on Messenger if you prefer to settle at the studio.

Are the prices promotional?

The strike-through figures are regular rates; the prices shown are the current promo. Promos can change, so the timetable is the final word.
