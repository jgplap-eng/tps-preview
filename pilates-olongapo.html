The nearest reformer studio

Reformer Pilates, a short drive away

Olongapo has gyms and group fitness, but no reformer studio of its own. Tessavi fills that gap from just across the gate: a ten to fifteen minute drive from Rizal Avenue, East Tapinac or Gordon Heights, with the Freeport's quieter roads for the last stretch.

Many of our clients live in Olongapo and train before work or after the school run. Early and evening slots are the first to fill, so book a few days ahead. If you are new, start with a Foundation class or a private session.

FAQ

Pilates near Olongapo City: common questions

Is there a Pilates studio in Olongapo City?

There is no reformer studio inside Olongapo City proper. Tessavi Pilates Studio, inside the Subic Bay Freeport Zone, is the nearest, about ten to fifteen minutes from the city centre.

Which gate should I use from Olongapo?

Enter through the Main Gate at the end of Magsaysay Drive or the Kalaklan Gate from Barretto, then follow the Freeport roads to WOW World Complex. Use the Waze or Google Maps buttons on this page for turn-by-turn directions.

Do you have classes after office hours?

Yes. Evening slots are listed on the live timetable and tend to book out first, so reserve early in the week.

How much does a class cost?

A single Group Reformer class is ₱1,100. Six-session packages are ₱5,700, valid for one month.
