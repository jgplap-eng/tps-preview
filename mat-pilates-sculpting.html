STOTT Mat Pilates®

Precise, controlled, form-focused

Precise, controlled and form-focused movement designed to build core strength, improve posture and deepen body awareness. Classes follow the STOTT PILATES® matwork repertoire, with props such as the mini ball, band and light weights used to assist or challenge each exercise.

Mat Pilates is where the fundamentals live: breath, pelvic placement, rib-cage control and shoulder stability. Learn them here and every reformer class gets better.

Core strengthPostureBody awarenessAll levels

Sculpting

Strength you can feel

Strength-focused movement using resistance to challenge your muscles, build endurance and support a stronger body. Expect higher repetitions with hand weights, resistance bands and the Pilates ball, structured so that core control never drops while the arms and legs work.

Sculpting pairs well with reformer training: two reformer classes and one Sculpting class a week is a complete programme for most clients.

FAQ

Mat and Sculpting questions

What is STOTT PILATES®?

A contemporary, anatomy-based approach to Pilates developed by Merrithew. It keeps the original principles but updates them with current exercise science, emphasising neutral spine, scapular stability and breath. Tessavi is the only studio in Subic offering it.

Do I need reformer experience for mat or sculpting classes?

No. Both are taught from the ground up. Mat work is a great place to learn core control; Sculpting adds light weights, bands and the ball for strength and endurance.

How are these different from the reformer?

Mat and Sculpting classes use body weight and small props instead of the reformer's springs. They complement reformer training and are a good option on days when reformer slots are full.
