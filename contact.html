Getting here

From the gates

From Olongapo: enter through the Main Gate (Magsaysay Drive) or the Kalaklan Gate and follow the Freeport roads to WOW World Complex. Olongapo guide.

From Bataan and SCTEX: exit at the Tipo Gate and continue into the Freeport. Bataan guide.

From Subic town and Castillejos: take the national highway south and enter through the Kalaklan or Main Gate. Zambales guide.

The studio is on the second floor of the complex. Look for the TESSAVI sign on the glass.
