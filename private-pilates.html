Private classes

Your body, your programme

A private session starts with how you move today. Your instructor assesses posture and mobility, then builds a reformer programme that targets what you need, whether that is a stronger back for long drives in from Olongapo, better hip mobility for golf at the Freeport courses, or a safe return to exercise after a break.

Sessions progress week to week, and because it is just you, the instructor can use the full studio: reformer, box, straps and props.

FAQ

Private session questions

Who are private sessions best for?

Anyone who wants a programme built around them: complete beginners who prefer one-on-one attention, clients returning after injury or pregnancy, athletes with a specific goal, and busy people who want a fixed weekly slot.

What is Classical Flow?

A structured one-on-one session based on the classical Pilates method, using flowing sequences in the traditional order to build strength, control and body awareness.

Can a Duo be two people of different levels?

Yes. The instructor adjusts springs and exercise choices for each person. It works well for couples and friends.

How do I book a private session?

Message us on Facebook Messenger with your preferred days and times, or call 0961 692 3951. Private slots are confirmed personally so we can pair you with the right instructor.

Do packages expire?

Single sessions and 6-session packages are valid for one month, 12 sessions for two months and 24 sessions for three months.
