Group Reformer

Small-group
reformer classes

A Reformer Pilates class limited to four clients, with one reformer per person, for a more focused and guided experience. Three levels, so you always train at the right intensity.

See the timetableAsk about levels

Foundation

Start with the essentials

Perfect for first-time clients and those new to Reformer Pilates.

You will learn:
  • Reformer setup and safety
  • Breathing and alignment
  • Core engagement
  • Control and proper technique
No previous Pilates experience is required.

All Levels

Build confidence and progress

Designed for clients with previous Reformer Pilates experience.

Expect:
  • A more continuous class flow
  • More movement combinations
  • Deeper cueing
  • Greater focus on technique and control
We recommend completing at least 2 Foundation classes before joining.

Intermediate

Challenge your control

Created for experienced clients who are confident on the Reformer.

Expect:
  • Faster and more fluid sequences
  • Greater strength and coordination
  • More complex movement combinations
  • Consistent muscle engagement
We recommend completing at least 10 All Levels classes before joining.

How it works

From booking to your first roll-down

Book a slot

Choose Foundation on the live timetable. Pay for a single class or a package at checkout.

Arrive ten minutes early

Progress on your terms

After two Foundation classes you are ready for All Levels. Your instructor will tell you when Intermediate makes sense.

FAQ

Group class questions

How many people are in a group class?

Four at most. Each client has their own reformer for the whole session.

Which level should I book first?

Foundation, unless you already have reformer experience elsewhere. After about two Foundation classes you can move to All Levels; after about ten All Levels classes, Intermediate.

How long is a class?

Plan for about an hour including setup and cool-down. Exact start times are on the live timetable.

Can I book a single class before buying a package?

Yes. A single Group Reformer class is ₱1,100. If you continue, a 6-session package brings the price to ₱950 per class.

What is your cancellation policy?

Please cancel through the booking system or Messenger as early as you can so the reformer can go to someone on the waitlist. Late-cancellation rules are shown at checkout.
